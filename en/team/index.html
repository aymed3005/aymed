Team

Project Team

  • Prof. Dr. Serdar Gülener Project Coordinator Bursa Uludağ University, Faculty of Economics and Administrative Sciences, Department of Political Science and Public Administration
  • Prof. Dr. Özlem Oğuzhan Researcher İstanbul Medeniyet University
  • Assoc. Prof. Dr. Gülden Kaya Uyanık Researcher Sakarya University
  • Buğra Kadir Koçer Fellow (doctoral student) Bursa Uludağ University

The team works according to the division of tasks in the project proposal. The coordinator is responsible for the research design, the codebook, approval of method decisions, and the analysis. The two researchers carry out the building of the news and decision archives, the matching protocols, and the control verification. The fellow carries out the pilot application of the coding guide and the independent human coding of 100 decisions.
