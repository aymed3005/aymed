Decisions

The 17,776 Constitutional Court decisions in the sampling frame (decision dates 23 September 2012 to 28 September 2025). Media coverage means at least one verified national news item within 180 days of the decision date. Subject summaries are in Turkish; they are derived from the decision texts.

Decision type

Counts are descriptive; the news archive covers online editions of national media and ends on 27 January 2026, so coverage for decisions after 31 July 2025 may be incomplete. For the coverage rule see Data › How Is Media Coverage Determined?
