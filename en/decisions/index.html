Decisions

The 19,794 Constitutional Court decisions in the frame (decision dates 23 September 2012 to 14 October 2026). Media coverage means at least one verified national news item between 7 days before the decision date and 30 days after the publication day. Subject summaries are in Turkish; they are derived from the decision texts.

Decision type

Counts are descriptive; the news archive covers online editions of national media and ends on 14 October 2026. For the 207 decisions published after 14 September 2026 the window has not yet closed, so their coverage may be incomplete. For the coverage rule see Data › How Is Media Coverage Determined?
