Limits of the news archive

The archive covers the online editions of the national media. Printed newspaper pages and the local press are not in it. If a decision has no news item in the archive, this does not mean it was mentioned nowhere. It means that no verified news item exists in the national online media, within the defined window.

Right truncation and open windows

The last news date in the archive is 14 October 2026. For decisions published after 14 September 2026, the 30 days after publication extend beyond this date, so coverage information for these decisions may be incomplete. These 207 decisions are flagged as having an “open window” and are kept out of the main analysis. Their windows will close as the archive is updated.

The X data describes a different period

The decision archive goes back to 2012, while the X data starts on 29 October 2024 and most of it was collected after August 2026. Decision news shared in that period mostly concerns 2026 decisions. For this reason, links between the X data and the decision archive concentrate on recent decisions. This results from the coverage of the data, not from the method.

Visibility and legal importance

Newsworthiness and legal importance are different things. A decision making the news does not show that it is more important legally. The project asks exactly which features of decisions produce this difference.

Coding scores involve interpretation

Open justice and mediatization criteria were scored on a 0-3 scale with anchor definitions. Two independent coders and an adjudicator were used, and agreement is reported as exact inter-coder agreement, weighted kappa, and Gwet AC2. Criteria whose agreement falls below the 0.80 threshold are flagged in the codebook and are tested separately in the sensitivity analyses. The scores do not say whether a decision is “good” or “bad”. They say how far a specific criterion is present in the text.

Year comparisons

The first and last years of the frame are incomplete: 2012 covers only the period after 23 September, and 2026 only decisions published up to 14 October 2026. Some 2026 decisions still have an open window. This should be kept in mind when reading years side by side.
