The project has produced three raw archives and one coded dataset. Figures are as of October 2026 and will change as the archive update continues.

1. Constitutional Court decision archive

Coverage Individual application decisions published in the Decisions Database (Kararlar Bilgi Bankası), from 23 September 2012, and constitutionality review decisions, from 1962. Sampling frame: 17,776 decisions (eligibility rule: decision date 23.09.2012 - 28.09.2025; first decision in the frame 27.09.2012, last decision 25.09.2025)
Size 22,795 decisions: 17,268 individual applications, 5,527 constitutionality reviews (cut-off: April 2026)
Content Full text and identification fields: application/docket number, decision date, deciding body (Plenary or Section), outcome, Official Gazette publication date, vote status, type of applicant
Source Constitutional Court Decisions Database (kararlarbilgibankasi.anayasa.gov.tr), decision documents in DOCX format

2. News archive

Coverage Online editions of national print media and national online-only news sites; news about the Court
Size 19,395 news items (January 2012 - 27 January 2026; collection cut-off 27 March 2026)
Source type Included traditional 7,986 · included digital 8,086 · agency 676 · Court institutional 276 · excluded (local, duplicate) 2,371
Subject label 2,629 news items concern a specific decision and are linked to a decision ID; the rest are general news about the Court or unrelated content
Content Headline, date, source, URL, full text, source type, subject label, linked decision ID

3. X (Twitter) dataset

Coverage Turkish-language posts about the Court and replies to posts by the Court’s official account
Size 12,132 posts (October 2024 - October 2026)
Labels Topic (decision news, individual application, legal debate, political comment, criticism, institutional announcement, other), sentiment, relevance, and suspected bot activity
Link to decisions Posts that report a decision are linked to a decision ID (October 2026: 145 posts, 100 decisions; this will grow with the archive update)

4. Coded decision dataset

Sample 500 decisions: 250 decisions covered in the media and 250 not covered, with the period distribution balanced
Criteria Codebook v4.3: 44 criteria (open justice 14, mediatization 23, identification and communication layer), each on a 0-3 scale
Evidence Each score comes with a verbatim quotation from the decision text; the quotations were verified by script
Additional layer For the 250 covered decisions, a visibility record: number of news items, number of independent sources, delay of the first news item, representative news item

The conditions for sharing the datasets are on the Data Access and Contributions page.
