The project has produced three raw archives and one coded dataset. Figures belong to the version of 16 October 2026.

1. Constitutional Court decision archive

Coverage Individual application decisions published in the Decisions Database (Kararlar Bilgi Bankası), from 23 September 2012, and constitutionality review decisions, from 1962; political party and High Court decisions in the Political Party Decisions Database, from 23 September 2012. Frame: 19,794 decisions (eligibility rule: decision date 23.09.2012 - 14.10.2026, undated and duplicate records excluded; first decision in the frame 27.09.2012, last decision 08.09.2026)
Size 23,459 decisions: 17,760 individual applications, 5,617 constitutionality reviews, 80 political party decisions, 2 High Court decisions (decisions published up to 14 October 2026; most recent decision 8 September 2026)
Identifier Decision number; the correction of 16 October 2026 separated 70 duplicate records and added 37 missing decisions
Content Full text and identification fields: application/docket number, decision date, deciding body (Plenary or Section), outcome, Official Gazette publication date, vote status, type of applicant
Source Constitutional Court Decisions Database (kararlarbilgibankasi.anayasa.gov.tr), decision documents in DOCX format; for some of the 37 added decisions the text was taken from hukukihaber.net, alomaliye.com or Lexpera, and the source is flagged
Political party and High Court Identification details from the Court’s Political Party Decisions Database, text from the Official Gazette (OCR for scanned pages); full texts for all 82 decisions (66 from the Official Gazette, 16 from the Court’s database and Lexpera). Political party decisions by type: deemed dissolved 49, name, emblem or removal from the register 11, warning 6, closure 1 (return of the HDP indictment), treasury 1, other miscellaneous 12. High Court: 2 decisions (2012 and 2017)
Financial audit layer 1,183 decisions on the financial audit of political parties (after 23 September 2012), identification details only; outside the frame and the media coverage count; news items are linked to 202 of them

2. News archive

Coverage Online editions of national print media and national online-only news sites; news about the Court
Size 29,714 raw records (January 2012 - 14 October 2026)
Source type Included 20,182: traditional 10,507 · digital 9,675 · agency 816 · Court website 318 · out of scope 8,398
Subject label 3,448 news items concern a specific decision and are linked to a decision ID (3,312 from the first labelling, 136 moved from general Court news during recoding); 8,922 items are general news about the Court (eight subclasses)
Content Headline, date, source, URL, full text, source type, subject label, linked decision ID

3. X (Twitter) dataset

Coverage Turkish-language posts about the Court and replies to posts by the Court’s official account
Size 10,512 posts (29 October 2024 - 14 October 2026)
Labels Topic (decision news, individual application, legal debate, political comment, criticism, institutional announcement, other), sentiment, relevance, and suspected bot activity
Link to decisions Posts that report a decision are linked to a decision ID (October 2026: 145 posts, 100 decisions; this will grow with the archive update)

4. Coded decision dataset

Design Full count (decision of 15 October 2026): all 19,794 decisions in the frame will be coded
Pilot 500 decisions (sample of 8 October 2026): 250 decisions covered in the media and 250 not covered, with the period distribution balanced
Criteria Codebook v4.3: 44 criteria (open justice 14, mediatization 23, identification and communication layer), each on a 0-3 scale
Evidence Each score comes with a verbatim quotation from the decision text; the quotations were verified by script
Additional layer For the 250 covered decisions in the pilot, a visibility record: number of news items, number of independent sources, delay of the first news item, representative news item

The conditions for sharing the datasets are on the Data Access and Contributions page.
