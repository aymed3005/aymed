Decision archive

Decisions were downloaded from the Constitutional Court Decisions Database as decision documents (DOCX). Each document was parsed into identification fields and merged into a single index. Decision date, deciding body, outcome, and Official Gazette publication date are extracted mechanically from the document text. Fields that cannot be extracted are marked “not found” and are not guessed.

Identifier correction and added decisions

On 16 October 2026 the identifier of every decision was tied to its decision number. This correction separated 70 duplicate records and added 37 decisions that were missing from the archive. For some of these, the text was taken from hukukihaber.net, alomaliye.com or Lexpera; the source is flagged in each record.

Political party and High Court decisions

On 16 October 2026, 80 political party decisions and 2 High Court (Yüce Divan) decisions were added to the archive and the frame. Identification details come from the Court’s Political Party Decisions Database and texts from the Official Gazette; scanned pages were read with OCR. Sixty-six texts come from the Official Gazette; the texts of 16 decisions not found there (14 political party, 2 High Court) were taken from the Court’s decisions database and Lexpera. All 82 decisions have a full text. Decisions on the financial audit of political parties (1,183 decisions) are kept, with identification details only, in a separate layer outside the frame.

News archive

News items were compiled by scanning the archives and RSS feeds of national news sites for content on the Constitutional Court. Each item first receives a source type label (traditional, digital, agency, Court institutional, excluded). Then the URL is resolved and the full text is retrieved. Finally a subject label is assigned (specific decision, general news about the Court, unrelated). Copies of the same news item on different sites are grouped as a “news family”. When source diversity is counted, one family counts as one independent source. On 15 October 2026 the archive was updated with news published up to 14 October 2026.

Link between news and decisions

Every news item about a specific decision is linked to that decision in the decision archive. The link is made in one of two ways. If the news item contains an application or docket number, the match is mechanical. If it does not, the identification details in the news text (applicant, subject, date, outcome) are used for a full-text search in the decision archive. Every link requires two independent clues and a quotation from the news item. Links with low confidence are set aside in a “queue” and are not counted. News items that mention the decision only before it was issued are kept separate.

Verification of control decisions in the pilot sample

For every decision labelled “not covered in the media”, the absence of coverage must be real. For this reason, each of the 250 control decisions drawn into the pilot sample of 8 October 2026 was searched again in the news archive by two separate routes (the decision’s identification details and subject keywords), following a protocol written in advance. The sensitivity of the search method was measured on 30 decisions known to be covered in the media, whose links had been hidden. Controls found to be mentioned in the archive were replaced with reserves from the same period.

X data

Posts were collected in regular rounds, first with a browser-based collector and then with a GraphQL-based one. Each post was labelled on the dimensions of topic, sentiment, and relevance. Posts that report a decision were linked to decisions under the same rule as in the news archive (two clues, a quotation as evidence).

Coding

Each of the 500 decisions in the pilot sample was scored on the 44 codebook criteria by two independent coders. Disagreements went to a third rater. The coders did not see whether a decision was covered in the media. From 15 October 2026, all decisions in the frame will be coded with the same codebook (full count).
