Time

The project’s analysis window starts on 23 September 2012. This is the day the individual application route to the Constitutional Court opened. The project examines individual application and constitutionality review decisions issued after that date. The decision archive itself is wider: constitutionality review decisions go back to 1962. The archive covers decisions published up to 14 October 2026 (version of 16 October 2026).

Decision types

There are four types of decisions: individual application (BB), constitutionality review (NRM, that is, annulment and referral), political party (SP) and High Court (Yüce Divan, YD) decisions. The archive holds 17,760 individual application, 5,617 constitutionality review, 80 political party and 2 High Court decisions. The project’s frame has 19,794 decisions: 17,735 individual applications, 1,977 constitutionality reviews, 80 political party and 2 High Court decisions. A decision enters the frame if its date falls between 23 September 2012 and 14 October 2026. Seventy-seven decisions without a decision date and 2 duplicate records are left out. The first decision in the frame is dated 27 September 2012 and the last 8 September 2026.

Media

The news archive covers two types of sources: the online editions of national traditional media (10,507 news items) and national news sites that publish only online (9,675 news items). These 20,182 items are the ones counted as “media coverage”. News agency output (816), announcements on the Court’s own website (318), and out-of-scope records (8,398) are kept in the archive with labels. They are not counted. The archive holds 29,714 raw records in total; news dates run from January 2012 to 14 October 2026.

Social media

Because of the platform’s restrictions on retrospective access, X (Twitter) data was collected going forward during the project period: 10,512 posts between 29 October 2024 and 14 October 2026. Most of the volume comes from regular collection rounds after August 2026. For this reason, the X data does not cover the same period as the decision archive.

What is out of scope

The paper pages of printed newspapers were not scanned; only online editions were collected. News images were not archived, so visual criteria could not be coded. Decisions on the financial audit of political parties (1,183 decisions) are kept, with identification details only, in a separate layer. They are not part of the frame or of the media coverage count. These decisions are account audits, they are numerous, and they do not fit the criteria of the codebook.
