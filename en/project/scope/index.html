Time

The project’s analysis window starts on 23 September 2012. This is the day the individual application route to the Constitutional Court opened. The project examines individual application and constitutionality review decisions issued after that date. The decision archive itself is wider: constitutionality review decisions go back to 1962. The first version of the archive was cut off in April 2026. Later decisions are being added through archive updates (see Announcements).

Decision types

There are two types of decisions: individual application (BB) decisions and constitutionality review (NRM, that is, annulment and referral) decisions. The archive holds 17,268 individual application decisions and 5,527 constitutionality review decisions. The project’s sampling frame has 17,776 decisions. A decision enters the frame if its date falls between 23 September 2012 and 28 September 2025. The first decision in the frame is dated 27 September 2012 and the last 25 September 2025.

Media

The news archive covers two types of sources: the online editions of national traditional media (7,986 news items) and national news sites that publish only online (8,086 news items). News agency output (676), announcements on the Court’s own website (276), and local or duplicate content (2,371) are kept in the archive with labels. They are not counted as “media coverage”. News dates run from January 2012 to 27 January 2026. The collection was cut off on 27 March 2026.

Social media

Because of the platform’s restrictions on retrospective access, X (Twitter) data was collected going forward during the project period: 12,132 posts between October 2024 and October 2026. Most of the volume comes from regular collection rounds after August 2026. For this reason, the X data does not cover the same period as the decision archive.

What is out of scope

The paper pages of printed newspapers were not scanned; only online editions were collected. News images were not archived, so visual criteria could not be coded. Political party closure cases, party financing audits, and High Court (Yüce Divan) decisions are outside the decision archive.
