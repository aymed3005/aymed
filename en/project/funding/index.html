  • Funding and Institutional Support

The project is funded by TÜBİTAK: 3005 Innovative Solutions in Social and Human Sciences Research Projects Support Programme, Project No 225K477, duration 18 months, start date 23 December 2025. The host institution is the Department of Political Science and Public Administration, Faculty of Economics and Administrative Sciences, Bursa Uludağ University. The researchers are from İstanbul Medeniyet University and Sakarya University.

Administrative processes run through the Scientific Research Projects unit of Bursa Uludağ University and the TÜBİTAK Project Tracking System. This site is published as a static site on GitHub Pages; the project’s institutional page is at uludag.edu.tr/aymed.

The views and assessments on this site belong to the project team. They do not reflect the official views of TÜBİTAK.
