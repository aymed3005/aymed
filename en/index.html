Media visibility of Constitutional Court decisions in Türkiye

Explore 19,794 decisions of the Constitutional Court of Türkiye issued since 23 September 2012, together with whether each one was covered by national news media. Decision details, outcome, constitutional articles and coverage status; filter them and go to the source.

Dataset metadata

Coverage
Constitutional Court individual application, constitutionality review, political party and High Court decisions; decision dates 23 September 2012 to 14 October 2026 (most recent decision 8 September 2026)
Frame
19,794 decisions; media coverage means at least one verified national news item between 7 days before the decision date and 30 days after the publication day
Sources
Constitutional Court Decisions Database (decision texts); the Court's Political Party Decisions Database and the Official Gazette (political party and High Court decisions); online editions of national print and digital media (news archive)
Version
2026-10-16; next update when the archive is completed

Announcements

  • 16 October 2026
    Data update (16 October 2026)

    The decision and news archives were updated to 14 October 2026. In the decision archive, the identifier was tied to the decision number: 70 duplicate records were separated and 37 missing decisions added. 80 political party and 2 High Court decisions were added to the archive and the frame; decisions on the financial audit of parties are kept in a separate layer outside the frame. The decision table now shows the full-count frame of 19,794 decisions. The media coverage window opens 7 days before the decision date and closes 30 days after the publication day.

  • 14 October 2026
    Data site launched

    The data site of AYMED (“Factors Determining the Media Visibility of Constitutional Court Decisions in Türkiye and a Model Proposal to Strengthen the Court's Communication with the Public”; TÜBİTAK 3005, Project No 225K477) is live: the Constitutional Court decisions in the frame, with a filterable table and charts. The project's institutional page will open at uludag.edu.tr/aymed.

  • October 2026
    Packages for human confirmation coding are ready

    The fellow will independently code 100 decisions from the 500-decision sample on 17 index components and verify 20 news-decision links. The coding packages and the guide are ready. Results will be reported in the first progress report as fellow-model agreement.
