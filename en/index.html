Media visibility of Constitutional Court decisions in Türkiye

Explore 17,776 decisions of the Constitutional Court of Türkiye issued since 23 September 2012, together with whether each one was covered by national news media. Decision details, outcome, constitutional articles and coverage status; filter them and go to the source.

Dataset metadata

Coverage
Constitutional Court individual application and constitutionality review decisions; decision dates 23 September 2012 to 28 September 2025
Frame
17,776 decisions; media coverage means at least one verified national news item within 180 days of the decision
Sources
Constitutional Court Decisions Database (decision texts); online editions of national print and digital media (news archive)
Version
2026-10-08; next update when the archive is completed

Announcements

  • 14 October 2026
    Data site launched

    The data site of AYMED (“Factors Determining the Media Visibility of Constitutional Court Decisions in Türkiye and a Model Proposal to Strengthen the Court's Communication with the Public”; TÜBİTAK 3005, Project No 225K477) is live: the 17,776 Constitutional Court decisions in the frame, with a filterable table and charts. The project's institutional page will open at uludag.edu.tr/aymed.

  • October 2026
    Packages for human confirmation coding are ready

    The fellow will independently code 100 decisions from the 500-decision sample on 17 index components and verify 20 news-decision links. The coding packages and the guide are ready. Results will be reported in the first progress report as fellow-model agreement.

  • October 2026
    Archive update is under way

    The decision archive is being updated with 2026 decisions, and the news archive with news items after 28 January 2026. When the update is finished, the linking of X posts to decisions will be run again and the figures on the Datasets page will be refreshed.
